Design → prediction → experiment

“We built Recursive Lab around a simple rule: an agent saying ‘done’ is not enough.

Nemotron runs on Crusoe and chooses what to do next. OpenCode gives it real tools and, in our task mode, native subagent delegation.

Our Python layer records the actions and checks actual outcomes. Failed attempts stay in the record, and feedback goes back to the model.

For this experiment, we gave Nemotron the actual framework source—not a planted bug or a supplied patch. It chooses what to investigate, writes a reproducer and makes its own change.

Our prediction is testable: you should see new model decisions, real tool execution and a verdict backed by tests. A plausible patch alone will not pass the gate.

Now let’s look at the real experiment. This tests autonomous investigation and verification; we are not claiming a proven self-upgrade.”
